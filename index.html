Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few details to help you get started.

Are my GIS files uploaded to GeoConvert?

The conversion workflow runs in your browser. Your selected source files are not sent to a GeoConvert application server. The page does load its conversion libraries from third-party content delivery networks.

Which formats can I convert?

The current version supports KML, KMZ, GeoPackage, Shapefile (as a ZIP or its component files), GeoJSON, DXF, and Excel/CSV coordinate tables. Some output formats have specific limitations shown in the converter.

Can I change the coordinate reference system?

Yes. Choose an output coordinate system in the converter. For Excel/CSV and DXF inputs, you also need to specify the source coordinate system because those formats do not reliably contain CRS information.

Is GeoConvert free?

Yes. This launch version is free to use and does not require an account. Paid plans and optional advertising may be considered in the future.

Why did my conversion fail?

Check that your input file is valid and, for Shapefiles, that the required companion files are included. If you are converting a table or DXF, make sure the source coordinate system and coordinate columns are correct.
